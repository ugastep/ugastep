Civic & Tax Uganda Revenue Authority (URA)

How to Register for a Non-Individual (Company/Business) URA Tax TIN

Estimated Cost 100% Free (Online via URSB Integration)
Processing Time 1 - 2 Working Days
Where to Apply URA Web Portal (www.ura.go.ug) / URSB Online Services System
Official Portal Open Official Portal

Overview

A mandatory corporate Tax Identification Number for registered Limited Liability Companies, Partnerships, NGOs, Cooperatives, and Trusts operating in Uganda.

Required Prerequisites & Documents

Valid URSB Certificate of Incorporation or Registration
Memorandum & Articles of Association (MEMARTS) or Partnership Deed
Valid Individual TINs and National IDs for at least two Company Directors
Official company email address and active corporate phone number
Physical business address details (Plot Number, Building Name, Street, District)

Step-by-Step Instructions

0/6 completed

Step 1: Access URA Non-Individual TIN Workspace

WHERE TO GO: Go to www.ura.go.ug -> Click 'e-Services' -> Select 'Non-Individual TIN Registration'.

WHAT TO DO: Click 'Start New Registration' to begin the corporate tax portal application.

WHAT'S NEXT: Move to Step 2 to enter your business incorporation details.

Step 2: Enter URSB Registration Number & Validate Business

WHERE TO GO: Business Registration Details section on the portal.

WHAT TO DO: Select your entity type (e.g., Limited Liability Company). Type your exact URSB Registration Number (Example: 80020000123456) and Incorporation Date. Click 'Validate'. The portal pulls verified company names and share structure from URSB databases.

WHAT'S NEXT: Move to Step 3 to add directors and your designated tax representative.

Step 3: Enter Directors' TINs & Designate Tax Representative

WHERE TO GO: Directors and Officers section.

WHAT TO DO: Input the active individual TINs and NINs of at least two company directors. Assign one director or qualified accountant as the official Tax Representative responsible for submitting monthly/annual tax filings.

WHAT'S NEXT: Move to Step 4 to specify physical company premises and financial dates.

Step 4: Provide Business Address & Financial Year Period

WHERE TO GO: Address and Accounting details section.

WHAT TO DO: Enter physical office location details (Example: Plot 12, Kampala Road, Kingdom Kampala Building, Nakawa Division). Select your financial accounting cycle (e.g., July 1st to June 30th).

WHAT'S NEXT: Move to Step 5 to review corporate entries and submit.

Step 5: Review & Submit Corporate Application (UGX 0 Cost)

WHERE TO GO: Application summary and declaration footer.

WHAT TO DO: Double-check that all director TINs and URSB data match your official company documents. Tick the legal declaration box, complete the captcha challenge, and click 'Submit'. Processing cost is free (UGX 0).

WHAT'S NEXT: Move to Step 6 to receive and print your Corporate TIN Certificate.

Step 6: Receive Corporate Credentials & Download Certificate

WHERE TO GO: Official company email inbox and www.ura.go.ug login dashboard.

WHAT TO DO: Once approved by URA (within 24 hours), check company email for your 10-digit Corporate TIN (Example: 5001234567) and portal password. Log in at www.ura.go.ug, go to 'My Certificates', and download the PDF for commercial bank account opening.
