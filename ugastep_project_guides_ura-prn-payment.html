Civic & Tax Uganda Revenue Authority (URA)

How to Generate a PRN & Pay Taxes on the URA Portal

Estimated Cost PRN Generation is Free (You pay only the tax liability or statutory fee)
Processing Time 5 Minutes
Where to Apply URA Portal / Mobile Money (*165# or *185#) / Commercial Banks nationwide
Official Portal Open Official Portal

Overview

A Payment Registration Number (PRN) is a mandatory 13-digit code generated on the URA website whenever you pay tax, stamp duty, motor vehicle fees, court fees, or penalties in Uganda.

Required Prerequisites & Documents

Your 10-digit URA TIN
Exact payment amount in Ugandan Shillings (UGX)
Mobile Money account (MTN/Airtel) or Bank account details

Step-by-Step Instructions

0/6 completed

Step 1: Open the URA Payment Registration Page

WHERE TO GO: Go to www.ura.go.ug.

WHAT TO DO: Click 'e-Services' on the top navigation bar, then click 'Payment Registration' (Generate PRN).

WHAT'S NEXT: The e-Payment portal will open. Move to Step 2 to identify yourself and choose the tax head.

Step 2: Enter Your TIN & Select Tax Head

WHERE TO GO: Taxpayer and Payment Type section.

WHAT TO DO: Type your 10-digit TIN (Example: 1012345678). Your name will display automatically. Click on the 'Tax Head' dropdown menu and select the payment reason (Example: Select 'Income Tax', 'Stamp Duty', 'Motor Vehicle Registration', or 'Local Service Tax').

WHAT'S NEXT: Move to Step 3 to specify the exact amount of money to pay.

Step 3: Enter Payment Amount & Select Mode of Payment

WHERE TO GO: Payment Details section.

WHAT TO DO: Type the exact amount in Shillings into the 'Amount' box (Example: 100000 for UGX 100,000). Under 'Payment Mode', select how you plan to complete payment: choose 'Mobile Money' or 'Bank Teller'.

WHAT'S NEXT: Move to Step 4 to generate your official 13-digit payment code.

Step 4: Click 'Submit' & Save Your 13-Digit PRN

WHERE TO GO: Screen output popup after clicking submit.

WHAT TO DO: Click the green 'Submit' button. The portal displays a payment slip containing a 13-digit reference code called a PRN (Example: 2240001234567). Write down this 13-digit number or take a screenshot.

WHAT'S NEXT: Move to Step 5 to execute the payment using your phone or bank.

Step 5: Execute Payment via Mobile Money or Bank Branch

WHERE TO GO: Mobile phone USSD or any commercial bank teller desk.

WHAT TO DO (Mobile Money):
• MTN: Dial *165# -> Select 'Pay Taxes / URA' -> Enter 13-digit PRN -> Confirm taxpayer name -> Enter PIN.
• Airtel: Dial *185# -> Select 'Pay Taxes / URA' -> Enter 13-digit PRN -> Confirm details -> Enter PIN.
WHAT TO DO (Bank): Walk into any commercial bank counter, present your 13-digit PRN, and hand over cash.

WHAT'S NEXT: Move to Step 6 to verify that URA received your money.

Step 6: Receive Confirmation SMS & Check Tax Ledger

WHERE TO GO: Your phone SMS and www.ura.go.ug login dashboard.

WHAT TO DO: Within 2 minutes, you will receive payment confirmation via SMS. Log into your URA account, go to 'My Tax Ledger', and verify that the payment status displays 'PAID'. Keep the SMS receipt for your records.
