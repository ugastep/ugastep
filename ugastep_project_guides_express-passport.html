How to Apply for Ordinary or Express Passport Step by Step in Uganda

Estimated Cost UGX 400,000 (Express) and UGX 250,000 Normal (Ordinary) Fee
Processing Time 3 - 5 Working Days
Where to Apply DCIC Headquarters, Kyambogo
Official Portal Open Official Portal

Overview

Step-by-step procedure to acquire a machine-readable or e-passport through the Ministry of Internal Affairs online portal.

Required Prerequisites & Documents

National Identity Card (NIN)
Recommendation letter from LC1 & GISO
Birth Certificate / Baptism Card
Bank Payment Receipt (PRN)

Step-by-Step Instructions

0/8 completed

Step 1: Access the Official Passport Portal

WHERE TO GO: Open your browser and navigate to www.passports.go.ug.

WHAT TO DO: Verify you are on the Ministry of Internal Affairs Directorate of Citizenship & Immigration Control portal.

WHAT'S NEXT: Click 'Apply for Passport' to start your application.

Step 2: Start New Application & Security Setup

WHAT TO DO:
• Select 'Apply for Passport' and click 'Start New Application'.
• Accept the terms and conditions and click 'START'.
• Input your valid phone number with country code (e.g., +256700000001) and a valid email address.
• Choose a security question, type your answer, solve the captcha, and click 'Save and Continue'.

Step 3: Choose Passport Type, Priority & Office

WHAT TO DO:
• Passport Type: Select 'Ordinary' (for general citizens), 'Diplomatic', or 'Service/Official'.
• Application Type: Select 'New Application', 'Renewal', 'Change of Details', 'Damaged', or 'Lost'.
• Priority: Choose 'Ordinary' (UGX 250,000, 1–2 weeks) or 'Express' (UGX 400,000, 2–3 days).
• Place of Submission & Collection: Select your preferred center (e.g., Central Office Kyambogo, Jinja, Gulu, Arua, Mbarara). Keeping submission and collection at the same office is recommended.

Step 4: Enter Personal & Origin Details

WHAT TO DO:
• Enter your Surname, Given Name, 14-character NIN (e.g., CM980471001GHK), Gender, and Date of Birth exactly as printed on your National ID.
• Fill in your Marital Status, Country & Place of Birth, and Occupation.
• Residential & Origin Address: Select your District (matching your National ID), Tribe, and names of grandparents/descendants, then click 'Save and Continue'.

Step 5: Citizenship, Spouse & Parents Details

WHAT TO DO:
• Citizenship: Select 'By Birth', 'By Registration', or 'By Naturalization'.
• Spouse: Enter spouse names if married (or leave blank if single).
• Parents Details: Enter full names, status (Alive/Deceased), NIN, birth place, district, and tribe for both your Father and Mother.

Step 6: Next of Kin & Recommender Details

WHAT TO DO:
• Next of Kin: Provide full names, contact details, and NIN of a trusted relative or friend.
• Recommender: Enter the details and NIN of a Ugandan citizen with a National ID who knows you (not an immediate family member).

Step 7: Generate PRN, Pay Fees & Schedule Appointment

WHAT TO DO:
• Review all entered details carefully and submit your application.
• Generate your Payment Registration Number (PRN) and pay via Mobile Money or any commercial bank.
• Return to www.passports.go.ug, click 'Schedule Appointment', enter your surname, date of birth, and application ID to book your biometric enrollment date at Kyambogo or your selected regional office.

Step 8: Biometric Enrollment & Passport Collection

WHAT TO DO:
• Attend your appointment with your printed application form, PRN payment receipt, original National ID, and recommender ID copy.
• Track status online at www.passports.go.ug under 'Check Status'. Once you receive an SMS notification that your passport is ready, visit your collection center with your National ID and receipt to pick it up.
