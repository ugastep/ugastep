Civic & Tax Uganda Revenue Authority (URA)

How to Recover Lost TIN or Reset URA Password

Estimated Cost 100% Free
Processing Time 3 - 5 Minutes
Where to Apply URA Web Portal (www.ura.go.ug)
Official Portal Open Official Portal

Overview

Recover your forgotten 10-digit URA Tax Identification Number or reset your e-Tax portal account password instantly using your registered email address or National ID.

Required Prerequisites & Documents

National Identification Number (NIN)
Registered mobile phone number or email inbox

Step-by-Step Instructions

0/3 completed

Step 1: Access URA Login / Recovery Page

WHERE TO GO: Go to www.ura.go.ug -> Click 'Login' -> Click 'Forgot Password?' or 'Find My TIN'.

WHAT TO DO: Choose whether you are recovering your TIN number or resetting your portal password.

WHAT'S NEXT: Move to Step 2 to enter your registered details.

Step 2: Enter National ID (NIN) & Request OTP

WHERE TO GO: Identity recovery form.

WHAT TO DO: Type your 14-character NIN (Example: CM90012345678A) and registered email or phone number. Click 'Send Reset Link / OTP'.

WHAT'S NEXT: Move to Step 3 to verify identity and set a new password.

Step 3: Enter OTP & Set New Password

WHERE TO GO: Email inbox / SMS and portal reset screen.

WHAT TO DO: Open the SMS/email sent by URA, copy the reset code, paste it into the portal prompt, and type a new secure password (must include letters, numbers, and a symbol like Ura#2026).

WHAT'S NEXT: Click 'Save Password'. You can now log into your URA portal account immediately.
