Transport & Travel Uganda Driver Licensing System (UDLS) / Ministry of Works

New Driving Permit Application (UDLS / SafeDEAL)

Estimated Cost UGX 220,000 - 415,000 (Based on validity selected: 1, 3, or 5 Years)
Processing Time 30 Days (Mandatory learner window) + 1 Day (Express printing)
Where to Apply Kyambogo / Regional Offices (Gulu, Mbarara, Mbale, Jinja, Arua, Fort Portal)
Official Portal Open Official Portal

Overview

How to transition from a medical check to a learner's permit, pass the IOV test, and obtain your full Ugandan driving license through authorized UDLS evaluation centers.

Required Prerequisites & Documents

National ID (NIN) or Valid Passport
Medical Examination Form UCDP1 (signed & stamped by doctor)
Proof of Enrollment from a licensed driving school
Valid 3-Month Learner Driving Permit
Certificate of Competence from IOV (stamped on permit)
URA Payment Receipt (PRN)

Step-by-Step Instructions

0/6 completed

Step 1: Undergo Eye Test & Medical Examination

WHAT TO DO: Download Medical Form UCDP1 from the UDLS portal, then visit an authorized medical doctor or optometrist to complete your vision and physical fitness evaluation.

EXAMPLE: Go to an approved clinic, pass the eye chart test, and get the medical practitioner to sign and officially stamp your UCDP1 form (valid for up to 6 months).

Step 2: Generate URA PRN & Get Learner's Permit

WHAT TO DO: Go to the URA portal (ura.go.ug), generate a Payment Registration Number (PRN) for a Learner Driving Permit (UGX 60,000 for Class B), pay via Mobile Money or bank, then visit UDLS for biometrics.

EXAMPLE: Choose Ministry of Works - Learner Permit, pay via MTN/Airtel Mobile Money, then bring your stamped UCDP1 form and National ID to UDLS Kyambogo to capture your photo and fingerprints for your 3-month Learner's Permit.

Step 3: Train at Driving School & Book IOV Test

WHAT TO DO: Enroll in an accredited driving school to take practical driving and road sign lessons, then have the school book your official police test.

EXAMPLE: Attend 2 to 4 weeks of driving practice with an instructor. Because individual applicants cannot self-book the IOV test, your driving school will officially register and schedule you for testing.

Step 4: Pass Inspectorate of Vehicles (IOV) Test

WHAT TO DO: Generate a URA PRN for the Inspectorate of Vehicles (IOV) test fee (UGX 25,000), pay it, and sit for the practical and road safety driving test with Uganda Police officers.

EXAMPLE: Go to the IOV testing yard with your instructor and Learner's Permit. Once you pass the parking and road maneuvers, the officer will stamp your Learner's Permit with the Certificate of Competence (valid for 12 months).

Step 5: Observe 30-Day Window & Pay Full Permit Fee

WHAT TO DO: Wait until at least 30 days have passed from the date your Learner's Permit was issued, then log onto the URA portal to generate a PRN for your full driving permit card based on desired validity and pay digitally.

FEES: 1 Year = UGX 135,000 | 3 Years = UGX 230,000 | 5 Years = UGX 330,000 (Class B). Cash is not accepted at UDLS counters.

Step 6: Visit Licensing Office for Biometrics & Collect Permit

WHAT TO DO: Take your original IOV-stamped Learner's Permit, National ID, and URA receipt to Kyambogo or a regional branch for final biometric verification and smart card issuance.

WHAT'S NEXT: Complete a fast fingerprint verification and collect your printed plastic Smart Driving Card within 30 to 60 minutes on the same day.
