Civic & Tax Uganda Revenue Authority (URA)

How to Register for an Individual URA Tax TIN

Estimated Cost 100% Free (No government or agent fees)
Processing Time 10 - 20 Minutes (Online)
Where to Apply URA Portal (www.ura.go.ug) or any physical URA Station / TaxHub nationwide
Official Portal Open Official Portal

Overview

A Tax Identification Number (TIN) is a unique 10-digit identity code issued free of charge by URA. You need it in Uganda to get formal employment, open a bank account, buy land/property worth over 50 Million UGX, register a company, or clear goods at customs.

Required Prerequisites & Documents

Ugandan Citizens: Valid National ID (NIN number)
Foreign Residents: Valid Passport + Work Permit / Alien ID
Refugees: UNHCR Refugee Card or OPM Attestation Letter
Active personal email address (must be able to access inbox immediately)
Active phone number (registered in taxpayer name for SMS OTPs)
Physical Address details (District, County, Sub-county, Parish, Village)

Step-by-Step Instructions

0/6 completed

Step 1: Go to the Official URA Website

WHERE TO GO: Open your web browser on a phone or computer and type 'www.ura.go.ug'.

WHAT TO DO: Look at the top menu bar, click on 'e-Services', and then click on 'TIN Registration' (or click the main button that says 'Instant TIN').

WHAT'S NEXT: The website will open a clean online registration form. Move to Step 2 to choose your tax category.

Step 2: Choose 'Individual' & Verify Your Email Address

WHERE TO GO: The taxpayer category section at the top of the form.

WHAT TO DO: Click on 'Individual'. Type your active email address (Example: john.kato@gmail.com) and click 'Send Code'. Open your email inbox in a new browser tab, copy the 6-digit security code sent by URA (Example: 849201), switch back to the URA page, type the code into the box, and click 'Verify'.

WHAT'S NEXT: Once your email is verified, the portal unlocks the identity verification section. Move to Step 3.

Step 3: Enter Your National ID (NIN) to Auto-Load Your Details

WHERE TO GO: The identity details section on the portal.

WHAT TO DO: Select 'Ugandan Citizen'. Look at your National ID card and type your 14-character NIN (Example: CM90012345678A) and your exact Date of Birth. Click 'Validate'. The portal will talk directly to NIRA to automatically bring up your official legal names, date of birth, and gender.

WHAT'S NEXT: Your personal details are now locked and verified against official government records. Move to Step 4 to fill in your contact and location details.

Step 4: Fill in Your Phone Number, Address & Income Source

WHERE TO GO: The contact, location, and employment fields.

WHAT TO DO: Enter your active phone number (Example: 0772000000). Select your primary source of income from the dropdown list (Example: Select 'Employment' if employed, or 'Business' if self-employed). Select your residence details from the drop-down menus: District (e.g., Kampala), County (e.g., Kyadondo), Sub-County (e.g., Nakawa), Parish, and Village. If employed, enter your employer's TIN to link PAYE taxes.

WHAT'S NEXT: Now that all required fields are complete, move to Step 5 to double-check and submit.

Step 5: Review Your Details and Click 'Submit' (UGX 0 Cost)

WHERE TO GO: Bottom of the registration page.

WHAT TO DO: Scroll back up to review all inputs for accuracy. Tick the declaration box stating 'I declare that the information provided is true'. Solve the security captcha challenge (tick 'I am not a robot') and click the green 'Submit' button. Cost: UGX 0. No payment or processing fees are required.

WHAT'S NEXT: The system will generate your TIN instantly or within 24 hours. Move to Step 6 to get your TIN certificate.

Step 6: Receive Your 10-Digit TIN & Download Your Certificate

WHERE TO GO: Your phone SMS, email inbox, and www.ura.go.ug login page.

WHAT TO DO: Check your phone and email. URA will send your new 10-digit TIN (Example: 1012345678) and temporary password. Re-open www.ura.go.ug, click 'Login' at the top right, enter your TIN and password, go to 'My Certificates', and click 'Download TIN Certificate' to print your PDF.

WHAT'S NEXT: You now have an active TIN! Keep your credentials safe for bank, employer, or business needs.

Common Issues & Troubleshooting

NIRA System Timeout Error

WHAT TO DO NEXT: If the site says NIRA is unavailable, servers are busy. Retry early in the morning (before 8 AM) or late evening, or visit a physical URA branch for manual assistance.

Email Already Registered Error

WHAT TO DO NEXT: Each email can only link to one TIN. Type a different email address or use account recovery if you registered previously.
