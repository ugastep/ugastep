Transport & Travel Uganda Revenue Authority (URA)

How to Transfer Motor Vehicle / Motorcycle Ownership

Estimated Cost Transfer Fees + Stamp Duty (1% of vehicle value, minimum UGX 30,000 PRN)
Processing Time 1 to 3 Working Days
Where to Apply URA Portal / Physical Vehicle Inspection Center
Official Portal Open Official Portal

Overview

Legal transfer of motor vehicle or motorcycle (boda-boda) registration logbook from a seller to a buyer in Uganda using URA e-Tax portal transfer PINs.

Required Prerequisites & Documents

Original Vehicle Logbook
Signed Transfer Form (Form 10) & Sales Agreement
Active URA TINs for both Seller and Buyer
Copies of National IDs for both parties

Step-by-Step Instructions

0/3 completed

Step 1: Seller Initiates Transfer on URA Portal

WHERE TO GO: Seller logs into www.ura.go.ug -> Click 'Motor Vehicle' -> Select 'Initiate Ownership Transfer'.

WHAT TO DO: Seller enters vehicle registration number (e.g., UBG 123X) and buyer's 10-digit TIN. System generates a Seller Transfer PIN.

WHAT'S NEXT: Move to Step 2 for buyer acceptance.

Step 2: Buyer Accepts Transfer & Generates PRN

WHERE TO GO: Buyer logs into www.ura.go.ug -> Click 'Motor Vehicle' -> Select 'Accept Ownership Transfer'.

WHAT TO DO: Buyer enters vehicle number and Seller Transfer PIN. Portal calculates statutory stamp duty (1%) and transfer fees, then generates a payment PRN.

WHAT'S NEXT: Move to Step 3 to pay the PRN fees.

Step 3: Pay Transfer Fees & Submit Logbook for Printing

WHERE TO GO: Mobile Money (*165#/*185#) or Bank -> Physical URA Vehicle Registry Center.

WHAT TO DO: Pay the PRN fee via Mobile Money or Bank. Take physical vehicle inspection report, original logbook, sales agreement, and payment receipts to the nearest URA vehicle licensing office (e.g., Nakawa / regional branch) to collect your new digital logbook.

WHAT'S NEXT: Ownership transfer is 100% complete!
