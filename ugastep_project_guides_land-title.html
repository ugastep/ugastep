Civic & Tax Ministry of Lands, Housing and Urban Development

Land Title Verification & Search at Ministry of Lands

Estimated Cost UGX 10,000 per Search
Processing Time 1 Working Day
Where to Apply MLHUD Zonal Offices / National Lands Information Portal
Official Portal Open Official Portal

Overview

Protect yourself from land fraud by conducting an official land search and title verification before purchasing property.

Required Prerequisites & Documents

Block and Plot Number / Copy of Title
National ID of the Searcher
Mobile Money or Bank Payment Receipt

Step-by-Step Instructions

0/4 completed

Step 1: Access the National Lands Information System

WHAT TO DO: Log onto the MLHUD e-services portal or visit the Ministry Zonal Office (MZO) records desk covering the district where the land is located.

Step 2: Provide Plot & Block Details

WHAT TO DO: Input the accurate County, Block number, Plot number, and District as printed on the copy of the Certificate of Title.

Step 3: Pay Official Search Fee (UGX 10,000)

WHAT TO DO: Generate a URA payment reference (PRN) and pay the statutory UGX 10,000 search fee via Mobile Money or approved commercial bank.

Step 4: Review Official Search Report

WHAT TO DO: Examine the signed official search statement confirming the registered proprietor, acreage, and whether there are any mortgages, caveats, or court encumbrances.
